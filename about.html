My Journey in SEO

My passion for SEO started during my Computer Science studies at Stanford University, where I discovered the fascinating intersection of technology and marketing. What began as a curiosity about how search engines work evolved into a career dedicated to helping businesses thrive online.

Over the past 8 years, I've had the privilege of working with diverse clients—from local startups to Fortune 500 companies—each presenting unique challenges that have shaped my expertise. My technical background allows me to dive deep into the complexities of modern SEO, while my strategic mindset ensures every optimization serves a business purpose.

I believe in sustainable, white-hat SEO practices that build long-term value. My approach combines technical excellence with content strategy, user experience optimization, and data-driven decision making.
